The Cave · Our promise

Provenance,
then patience.

Every arowana that crosses our threshold is identified, isolated, and observed for twenty-one days before it joins the gallery. We do not sell a fish until we would keep it ourselves.

Provenance

Every fish carries a microchip, a CITES certificate, and our hand-written lineage card.

Quarantine

21 days of observation in isolated systems before any specimen joins the gallery.

Husbandry

Tank parameters monitored daily. Diet planned per specimen. We sweat the small things.

Continuity

We answer the phone five years after the sale. Your fish has a long life to live.
